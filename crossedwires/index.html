How it plays

Easy to learn. Satisfying when it snaps.

No typing and no letter grid. You connect pairs, and the board tells you immediately.

  1. 1

    Find the partner

    Words or pictures sit in two columns. Kettle belongs with tea. A router belongs with a laptop. Every tile has exactly one match.

  2. 2

    Draw the wire

    Tap the two tiles, or drag from one to the other. A correct wire glows and stays. The lines are allowed to cross on their way across.

  3. 3

    Mind the misses

    A wrong wire flashes and costs a try. Clear every pair before you run out. On a perfect round, the board celebrates.

The wires have a look

Each board dresses for the puzzle

The same match can feel like a circuit, a fuse, a vine, a studio cable, or a docking tether. Finish the board and the celebration matches the theme.

Some puzzles use pictures instead of words, or a mix of both. A snack board, a holiday icon set, a desk full of gear.

Three ways to play

A daily, a library, and five Journeys

  • Today. One official puzzle a day, the same one for everyone, with a streak that grows when you come back.
  • Practice. Hundreds of extra puzzles. Play them untimed, or on a clock. Browse by topic and jump back to any one you've tried.
  • Journey. Themed ladders. You clear a stage, and the next one unlocks. Twelve stages each, from everyday words up to the hardest boards in the game.

  • The Starting Line Free

    Everyday connections. A good place to begin. 12 stages.

  • The Workshop Pro

    How things are made. Tools, cooking, and a spark-trail finale. 12 stages.

  • The Wild Pro

    From ocean floors to mountain peaks. The glasshouse is the last stage. 12 stages.

  • The Lab Pro

    Science is matching what you measure to what it means. Circuits and orbits. 12 stages.

  • The Studio Pro

    Art, music, and performance. The hardest things to categorize. 12 stages.

The little things

Short rounds, with room to get stuck

  • Hints, includedOne hint on the daily, three in a Practice round, one on a Journey stage. Hints are not a separate purchase.
  • Memory challengeLook at the board, then the labels hide and you connect the pairs from what you remember. The wires stay visible.
  • A tile that doesn't belongHarder boards slip in a decoy. It looks plausible and has no partner.
  • Streaks and statsA daily streak, your best scores, and a share of how the round went.
  • ArchiveA calendar of past dailies. The last 7 days are free. Older days open with Pro. Archive wins do not change your streak.
  • Sound and hapticsA wire landing has a click and a tap. Music and effects each have their own switch, and Reduce Motion calms the celebrations.
  • Light, Dark, or SystemThe board follows the appearance you pick in Settings. A short tutorial on the real board teaches the first wires.
  • No account, no ads, no trackingProgress stays on the device. Nothing is sent off the phone. Privacy policy
  • Made for iPhoneA first-launch tutorial you can replay from Settings, and a welcome that offers Today, Practice, or Journey.
