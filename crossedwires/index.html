Crossed Wires

A matching puzzle for iPhone

Connect each word or picture on the left to its partner on the right, drawing glowing wires between them. Sooner or later two wires cross, and a wrong match costs you. A new puzzle every day, a practice library, and guided Journeys that grow harder as you go.

Status

Crossed Wires is in early testing and is not on the App Store yet. Questions or want to try it? ddworkin.apps@icloud.com.
