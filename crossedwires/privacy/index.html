Privacy Policy

Crossed Wires · Effective November 5, 2026

Crossed Wires does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing about how you play leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (puzzles solved, streaks, stats, and settings) is stored on your device only. It is not uploaded anywhere and is not synced between devices.

Purchases

Crossed Wires Pro, if offered, is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. I never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Third parties

Crossed Wires includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Children

Crossed Wires collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
