Sixteen words. Four stories. Put each in order.

Swap neighbouring tiles. A word glows when it touches the one it leads to.

Each word leads to the next.

Touchstone is a daily word puzzle. A board of tiles hides four chains of four words, each a little story, like spark → flame → smoke → ash. Swap neighbouring tiles until every chain is in order.

Try the puzzle on this page. It takes about three minutes.

  1. 1Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it to swap them. (Or drag a tile onto its neighbour.)
  2. 2A word lights up when it touches the word it leads to or follows. Order matters: the chain must run start to finish.
  3. 3Complete a chain and it clears into a row. The board shrinks, and the last chain is the hardest.

One new puzzle a day, the same for everyone, easy on Monday and harder as the week goes on. The full game adds the rest:

A new puzzle every day

Everyone plays the same board. Mondays are gentle; the week ramps up to Sunday, then starts again.

🔥

Streaks and a Streak Shield

Keep your streak going. A free monthly Streak Shield covers a day you miss.

🌱

First Steps

Six easy warm-up puzzles to learn the game without pressure, then jump into the daily.

🗂️

Ten collections

Themed sets of puzzles, each with its own look and its own mix of boards.

📚

The Daily Archive

Missed a day? Recent puzzles are free to play, and older ones come with Pro.

🔷

Shapes beyond chains

Hub, branch, star and cycle puzzles: a word in the middle with others around it, a chain that splits, or a loop. Walkthroughs show you each one.

🪨

Stone boards

Some boards have stones. Movable stones shift when you swap and never link; fixed walls never move, so you slide words into the gaps.

⚡

Rush mode

A fast 90-second mode where you clear as many chains as you can. One free run a day.

📈

Stats, replay and sharing

Watch your last solve replayed, replay a puzzle to beat your move count, and share your result.

📱

Widget, Siri and reminders

A Home Screen widget, "Play today" with Siri or Shortcuts, and an optional daily reminder.
