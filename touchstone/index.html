How it plays

Easy to learn. Satisfying to solve.

No typing, no timer, no guessing letters. Just you, sixteen words, and a few hidden chains.

  1. 1

    Spot the chains

    Most puzzles hide four chains of four words, each one step after the last, like DIRTY → WASH → DRY → FOLD. Every word belongs to exactly one.

    A fresh board. FLOWER and FRUIT already touch, so they start out glowing.

  2. 2

    Slide them together

    Swap neighboring tiles or drag a word along a path. When two linked words touch, they glow and join up.

    One swap: CUP trades places with SEED, lands next to TEA, and the pair lights up.

  3. 3

    Clear the board

    Finish a chain and it bursts, then settles into a solved row at the bottom. Clear them all to win.

    SEED → SPROUT → FLOWER → FRUIT is complete, so it clears into a solved row.

A new puzzle every day

Your three-minute morning ritual

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, so you can compare notes with friends.

  • Easy Monday, hardest Sunday. The week ramps up, so there's always a warm-up and always a challenge.
  • Streaks with a flame that grows every day you play.
  • Share your solve as a colorful card with your move count.
  • "Why these words?" After you solve, see how each chain connects.
  • Watch your last solve replayed move by move.

Not just straight lines

Special shapes and stone mazes

Some days the chain isn't a line. A hub word connects to three others; a cycle loops back on itself. And on stone days, you slide words through a maze of fixed stones.

Seasons and holidays

Boards that dress for the occasion

Themed puzzles bring their own look: rolling hills for a day outside, frost for a snow day, warm wood for the workshop, and special skins for holidays through the year.
