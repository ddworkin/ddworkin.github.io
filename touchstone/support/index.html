Support

Touchstone: Daily Word Puzzle

Email ddworkin.apps@icloud.com and I'll get back to you. If it's about a specific puzzle, please include its date.

I bought Pro but it's locked

Open the Archive, tap the unlock banner at the top, then tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Getting my progress on a new iPhone or iPad

Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud turned on. Touchstone syncs your streaks and solves automatically; on a fresh install it can take a minute after first launch for your history to appear.
