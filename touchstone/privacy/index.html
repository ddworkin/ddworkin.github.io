Privacy Policy

Touchstone: Daily Word Puzzle · Effective October 7, 2026

Touchstone does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics sent off your device. I can't see who plays or how.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (puzzles solved, streaks, stats, and settings) is stored on your device. The Touchstone home screen widget reads the same progress, on the same device, to show today's puzzle and your streak.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Touchstone keeps your progress in step across your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data lives in your iCloud account, is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to me. If you turn off iCloud for Touchstone, the app works the same and keeps progress on that device only.

Purchases

Touchstone Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. I never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Notifications, Siri, and widgets

Optional daily reminders are scheduled by the app on your device; there is no notification server. Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight, and widget features are handled by iOS on your device.

Third parties

Touchstone contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and shares no data with anyone.

Children

Touchstone collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting any data (for example, anonymous usage statistics to improve the puzzles), this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
