Privacy Policy

Touchstone: Daily Word Puzzle · Effective October 17, 2026

Touchstone does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as which puzzles get solved or abandoned, so I can improve the puzzles. I can't see who you are, and you can turn this off in Settings.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (puzzles solved, streaks, stats, and settings) is stored on your device. The Touchstone home screen widget reads the same progress, on the same device, to show today's puzzle and your streak.

Anonymous usage statistics

Touchstone sends small, anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Examples: that a puzzle was started, a group was cleared, a puzzle was solved or left unfinished, how many moves or how long it took, whether undo was used, and whether a Pro screen was opened or dismissed. The service also adds basic information about the app and device: app version, iOS version, device model, language and region, time zone, display and accessibility settings, and simple counts such as how many days the app has been used.

These events are tied only to a random-looking identifier produced by scrambling an app-specific device identifier with a one-way hash. They are not linked to your name, email, or Apple Account, are not used to track you across other apps or websites, and are not sold or shared for advertising.

To stop sending them, turn off Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data in the app.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Touchstone keeps your progress in step across your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data lives in your iCloud account, is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to me. If you turn off iCloud for Touchstone, the app works the same and keeps progress on that device only.

Purchases

Touchstone Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. I never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Notifications, Siri, and widgets

Optional daily reminders are scheduled by the app on your device; there is no notification server. Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight, and widget features are handled by iOS on your device.

Third parties

Touchstone includes one third-party component, the TelemetryDeck analytics library, used only for the anonymous usage statistics described above. It contains no advertising or tracking code, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.

Children

Touchstone collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage statistics contain no personal information and can be turned off in Settings.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
